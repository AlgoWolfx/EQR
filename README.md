# EQR

**Free, private and open-source QR code studio.**

EQR is a fast QR code generator focused on privacy, clean UX and useful everyday QR formats.

> Free. Open source. Your data stays on your device.

## Planned features

- URL QR codes
- Wi-Fi QR codes
- vCard/contact QR codes
- WhatsApp, email and phone QR codes
- Location QR codes
- Google Review QR codes
- Custom colors and error-correction level
- Optional logo/embed
- PNG and SVG export
- Printable QR sheets
- Batch QR generation
- Light and dark themes
- No account required
- Local-first processing

## Product principles

- No unnecessary backend
- No tracking required for core functionality
- Fast enough to feel instant
- Accessible and keyboard-friendly
- Simple enough for anyone to use
- Professional enough for business use
