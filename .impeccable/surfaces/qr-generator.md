# QR generator surface contract

Mode: Operate, with secondary Read content below the working tool.
Build path: code, explicitly chosen by the user.
Direction: user-pinned Apple-inspired utility restraint. No comp was requested.

## First viewport

Small EGORA/EQR header. One H1: Free QR Code Generator. Two short supporting lines.
Compact real QR type controls, primary input, live preview and downloads. No
decorative hero, illustrations, gradients, serif display type or marketing cards.
URL is the default; mobile keeps input first and shows preview directly after the
optional customization disclosure. All existing capabilities remain available.

## Visual and interaction contract

System font, near-black text, warm neutral surfaces, restrained green accent.
Hierarchy comes from spacing and typography. Only preview and input controls
need boundaries; the layout itself has no cards. Press feedback is immediate
with 0.97 scale over 160ms; reduced motion removes scale. Hover is capability gated.
The signature interaction is the actual preview updating with each edit, with no
artificial delay or required generation step. Inputs and actions remain usable
with keyboard, touch and 200% text sizing.

## Content and SEO contract

Working generator before useful English SEO content. Six concise use cases,
three-step guide, static/dynamic distinction and ten native FAQ disclosures.
Visible FAQ and JSON-LD share one source. No invented proof or ratings.
Static codes do not expire; linked destinations can disappear. Generation has
no lifetime quota, but batch ZIPs are limited to 50 items per operation.
HTML must contain the guide and FAQ without JavaScript. Production URL remains
unset by user choice; preview blocks indexing and omits guessed canonicals.

## Removed complexity

Decorative heading sparkles, tilted QR paper, purple preview, sidebar artwork,
step badges, repeated benefits and custom font downloads. Customization is
disclosed on demand. JSZip is loaded only for a ZIP operation.

## Evidence and known limits

Browser automation and independent QR decoders verify functionality.
Physical phones, printed paper, deployed Lighthouse and real Search Console
results require a deployment or hardware. No such results are claimed here.
