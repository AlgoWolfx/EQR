# EQR by EGORA Digital

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People arriving from search who need to create and download a QR code quickly.

## Product Purpose

A free static QR code generator. Success is entering information, downloading a
working code, and getting on with the day. English remains the source and fallback
language. The user requested German, Turkish, Arabic, Japanese, Spanish and Portuguese
through a simple language selector; Arabic uses RTL.
QR content is never translated.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the working React, TypeScript, Vite and browser-local QR implementation.
Keep all ten formats, customization, logo upload, PNG/SVG exports, clipboard,
batch ZIP, printing, dark mode and offline use. No accounts, watermark, payments,
analytics, backend or dynamic QR infrastructure. Batch exports contain up to 50
items; there is no lifetime generation quota. Codes do not expire, but their
external destinations can disappear. Production URL is deliberately undecided;
keep it configurable. The user will integrate this tool with their main website.

## Brand Commitments

Apple-inspired utility principles and Emil Kowalski interaction quality, with
Impeccable for copy, hierarchy and finish. System UI fonts are explicitly required.
Quiet, precise, simple, restrained. The generator leads; SEO depth follows.
EGORA Digital branding is subtle. No fake ratings, testimonials or usage numbers.
No decoration, elaborate animations or SaaS landing-page conventions.

## Product Principles

1. Utility before marketing.
2. Privacy through local generation.
3. Speed and clear feedback before visual effects.
4. Useful, honest SEO content before keyword volume.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, good contrast, touch-friendly controls, support
320px screens and 200% text scaling. Respect reduced motion and browser zoom.
